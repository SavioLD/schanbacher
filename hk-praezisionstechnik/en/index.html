How we work

No queue. A clear direction.

  1. Straight to the core team

    When an enquiry reaches us, it doesn’t end up in a queue. It goes straight to a small core team of design and application engineers – people who can make technical decisions and are mandated to do so.

  2. A reliable direction within a few days

    No declaration of intent, no interim status – a concrete statement you can use for your internal planning. While other suppliers are still aligning internally, you already have a basis to work with.

  3. Once you commit, we start

    Without waiting until every contract clause has been negotiated down to the last detail. Because your project has a deadline.

A missed start of production, a postponed OEM approval or a delayed market launch has consequences that no good supplier contract can make up for.
